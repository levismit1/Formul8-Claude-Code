# Refund & Return Policy

**Formul8 Nutrition | 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

At Formul8 Nutrition, your satisfaction is our priority. We believe in the quality of our products and want you to feel confident with every purchase. That's why we offer a 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee.

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, we're here to make things right.

## 1. Our 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on purchases made directly through our official website.

If you're not completely satisfied with your Formul8 Nutrition product, you may request a refund within 30 days of receiving your order.

## 2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our customer support team at support@formul8nutrition.com.

Please include:

* Your full name
* Your order number
* The email address associated with your purchase

Our customer support team will review your request and provide instructions for the next steps.

## 3. Return Eligibility

To qualify for our 30-day money-back guarantee:

* Your purchase must have been made directly through the Formul8 Nutrition website.
* Your refund request must be submitted within 30 days of delivery.
* You must provide a valid order number or proof of purchase.

Opened or partially used products are eligible for our money-back guarantee. We want you to have the opportunity to try Formul8 Nutrition with confidence.

## 4. Refund Processing

Once your refund request has been approved, your refund will be issued to your original payment method.

Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear in your account, depending on your bank or payment provider.

## 5. Shipping Costs

Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless your order arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect.

If a physical return is required, our customer support team will provide instructions before you ship anything.

## 6. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us as soon as possible.

We will work with you to arrange an appropriate replacement or refund at no additional cost.

## 7. Subscription Orders

If you subscribe to Formul8 Nutrition, each eligible delivery is covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee.

Refund requests do not automatically cancel future subscription orders. To stop upcoming charges, you must cancel your subscription separately through your customer account or by contacting our support team.
